## Data services & web scraping

- **Custom scrapers and crawlers** - extraction tailored to your sources, required fields and update schedules, including websites with no API.
- **Data pipelines** - collection, cleaning, normalisation and enrichment across websites, APIs, databases and document stores.
- **Monitoring and recurring collection** - scheduled extraction to track changes in listings, prices, content and other business data.
- **Data delivery and integration** - structured datasets connected to your warehouse, dashboards, search infrastructure or AI systems.
- **Web & mobile engineering around data** - dashboards, internal tools and customer-facing web and mobile applications built on top of your data pipelines and AI systems.

## AI development & consulting

- **Agentic apps and multi-agent systems** - agents that call tools, use structured outputs and execute workflows across your business systems.
- **RAG and knowledge systems** - retrieval over your own data and relevant web sources, with chunking, embeddings, vector stores and evaluation of answer accuracy.
- **AI-powered data workflows** - document classification, information extraction, triage, routing and reporting across large datasets.
- **Model selection and orchestration** - the right model for each task, with production experience across Anthropic, OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex AI and xAI (Grok).

We connect agents to data sources through APIs and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), including custom servers and tools. Our stack includes Vercel AI SDK and Mastra. Access controls are enforced by the underlying application and data layers, with moderation and validation gates for model outputs and deterministic code for operations that must be exact.

We take these systems through deployment and monitoring, including the API, dashboard or conversational interface needed to use them.
